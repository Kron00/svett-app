# Using svett

svett logs lifting and cardio in one place. A lifting session, a run, a ride, a walk and a hike all land in one history. This guide walks through each tab and what you can do there.

## The first time you open svett

svett opens on four short pages. They say what svett is, that your data stays on your phone, that an account is optional, and that the coach runs on your own ChatGPT plan. Tap **Next** to read on, or **Skip** at any time. Either way you land on Train, and svett does not show the pages again.

svett asks for nothing on these pages. It asks for each permission only when you first use the feature that needs it:

- notifications, when your first rest timer starts;
- location, when you first record a run, ride, walk or hike;
- nothing for Apple Health, because svett reads Health's export file instead.

## The tabs

svett has five tabs. A workout in progress shows as a bar above them on every tab. Tap the bar to go back to it.

| Tab      | What it holds                                                                 |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home     | Your feed: your workouts and those of the people you follow.                  |
| Train    | Start a workout or record cardio. Your routines and the exercise library.     |
| Progress | History, the calendar, records, charts, muscles and body measurements.        |
| Coach    | A chat with your coach, which runs on your ChatGPT plan.                      |
| You      | Your account and profile, ChatGPT, your data, units and plates, phone checks. |

## Train

### Start a workout

Tap **Start empty workout**, or open a routine and tap **Start workout**. While you lift:

- Tap a weight or reps field and type on the keypad. Last time's values show beside each set, and an empty set takes them when you mark it done.
- Tap the box at the end of a set to mark it done. Its rest timer starts. svett tells you with a notification when the rest is over, even with the phone locked.
- Tap the set number to make it a warm-up, failure or drop set.
- Open an exercise's options to add a note, change its rest, join it with the next one in a superset, move it, replace it or remove it.
- The warm-up calculator suggests warm-up sets for your working weight. The plate calculator shows which plates go on each side of the bar.
- Turn on RPE in **You > Units and plates** to rate how hard each set was.

Tap **Finish** to save the workout. It goes to your history. **Discard workout** throws it away.

### Routines and the exercise library

Routines are saved plans: the exercises, their sets and their targets. Group them in folders, such as a three-day split. Tap **New routine** to make one, or save a finished workout as a routine from its detail screen. There is no limit on routines.

The exercise library lists over 800 exercises with their muscles and equipment. Search by name, or filter by muscle or equipment. If an exercise is missing, tap **New exercise** to make your own.

### Cardio

Tap **Record cardio** to track a run, ride, walk or hike with GPS. svett keeps recording with the screen locked. When you finish you see the route on a map, your splits and the climb. Tap **Add cardio by hand** to enter a session, such as a swim or a class.

## Progress

Progress shows your streak, then ways into the calendar, charts, muscles and measurements, your newest records, and every workout, newest first.

- **History.** Tap a workout to see every set. You can change a set, save the workout as a routine, or delete it. Undo brings a deleted workout back.
- **Calendar.** A month of workouts, with your streak. The streak counts weeks, Monday to Sunday, with at least one workout.
- **Records.** A set becomes a record when it beats every earlier workout with that exercise. Each record says which rule it beat: heaviest weight, estimated one-rep max, best set volume, most reps or longest time.
- **Exercises.** A chart for each exercise you have logged, over the range you pick.
- **Muscles.** Sets per muscle each week. The screen says how it counts a set, so you know where the number comes from.
- **Body measurements.** Body weight, body fat and tape measurements, each on its own chart. Only you can see them. They never appear in the feed.

When there is nothing to show yet, each screen says what goes there and offers the step that fills it.

## Coach

The coach answers questions about your training, such as "What was my best squat set last month?". It reads your data on the phone through its tools, and each answer says which of your data it read.

To turn it on, tap **Continue with ChatGPT** on the Coach tab and sign in to ChatGPT. The coach uses your ChatGPT plan, so it costs nothing on top of it. ChatGPT lists Go, Plus, Pro and Business as plans that allow this. A Free account cannot use the coach. Everything else in svett works without it.

- The coach can draft a routine or a program. It shows as a preview under the answer. Nothing is saved until you tap **Apply**, and **Undo** takes it away again.
- Your chats stay on your phone. They are part of your export and never sync.
- When your plan's limit is reached, the coach says so and points you to ChatGPT's usage settings. svett never pays for a request another way.
- Manage the sign-in in **You > ChatGPT**. Signing out deletes the sign-in from the phone and tells OpenAI to revoke it. A sign-in you have not used for 30 days lapses, and you tap Continue with ChatGPT again.

## Your data: export and import

Open **You > Your data**.

- **Export.** One file with every workout, routine, measurement and coach chat. Keep it as a backup, or move it to another phone.
- **Import a svett file.** Bring back an export. You see what it adds and changes before anything is saved. Workouts you already have are skipped, and where your copy and the file's differ, svett keeps yours unless you switch on the file's version.
- **Import from Hevy.** In Hevy, open Settings, then Export & Import Data, and export your workouts. Pick that file in svett. svett matches Hevy's exercise names to its library and asks about any it does not know. It remembers your answers. You can import the same file again later, and svett adds only what is new.
- **Apple Health and GPX.** In the Health app, tap your picture, then Export All Health Data, and pick the export.zip in svett. svett shows the workouts it found and asks before it saves any. A GPX file from another app works the same way.

Every import can be undone. Open it under **Past imports** on the same screen and tap **Undo import**.

## Privacy and accounts

You do not need an account. Logging, history, progress, the coach, export and import all work without one, and offline.

An account adds two things: sync between your phones, and the feed. To make one, open **You > Account** and tap **Continue with ChatGPT** or **Continue with email**. svett emails you a six-digit code each time you sign in. A sign-in lasts 7 days. After that svett asks for a new code before it syncs again, and everything on the phone keeps working meanwhile.

The first time you sign in, svett asks whether to move this phone's data into the account. If you say no, the account starts empty and your earlier data stays on the phone for when you sign out. Each account's data stays separate on the phone.

What others see:

- Each workout has an audience: everyone, your followers, or only you. New workouts start as only you, and so does everything you logged before the account and everything you import. You can change your default and each workout's audience.
- A workout can hide its weights, heart rate, start time, calories and pace.
- A route can hide its first and last stretch, so the map does not show where you live.
- A private profile means you approve each follower.
- Blocking someone means neither of you sees the other, and any follow between you ends. You can unblock them under **Blocked accounts** on your profile.
- Body measurements never appear in the feed.

## The feed

With an account, Home shows your workouts and those of people you follow. A lifting session shows its exercises and sets, and a run its map, distance and pace, each with what its owner chose to share. Like a post or leave a comment. Tap **Find people** to search by username. The inbox lists likes, comments, follows and follow requests.

## Deleting your account

Open **You > Account** and tap **Delete account**. svett deletes everything it keeps for you on its server and signs you out on every phone. It then asks what happens to the copy on this phone:

- **Keep a copy on this phone** keeps your workouts here, with no account.
- **Erase it from this phone** removes them too. This cannot be undone.

Export your data first if you want a copy you can import later.

## Phone checks

**You > Phone checks** tests what only a real phone can show: the Keychain after a restart, a notification in the background, GPS with the screen locked, Apple Health permission and the ChatGPT sign-in. **Copy report** puts the results on the clipboard. The report never contains a token.
